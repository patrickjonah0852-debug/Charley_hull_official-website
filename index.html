MY STORY

About Me

Charley Hull is an English professional golfer known for her fast pace, fearless attitude and rapid rise through the professional ranks.

Born on March 20, 1996, in Kettering, England, she started playing golf at a young age and developed a passion for the sport.

She turned professional in 2013 at the age of 17 and quickly established herself as a competitor on the international golf stage.

Career Highlights

Important milestones in professional golf.

Early Beginnings

Started playing golf at a young age at Kettering Golf Club in England.

2013 - Turning Professional

Turned professional at age 17 and earned the Ladies European Tour Rookie of the Year award.

2014 - European Success

Won her first professional event in Morocco and the Ladies European Tour Order of Merit.

Solheim Cup

Made her Solheim Cup debut in 2013 and became an important member of Team Europe.

Olympic Games

Represented Great Britain at the Rio 2016 and Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

LPGA Tour Achievements

Career victories include the 2016 CME Group Tour Championship, 2022 The Ascendant LPGA and 2025 Kroger Queen City Championship.

Major Championships

Has achieved multiple runner-up finishes in major championships.
